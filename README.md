# All Star Tower Rater

All Star Tower Rater is a tool designed to enhance the (pro) experience of playing All Star Tower Defense on Roblox.
It offers advanced features like unit comparison, visual stats, and team rating to help players optimize their team building.

## Features

- **Advanced Unit Search Engine**: Quickly search for units based on various attributes and stats.
- **Unit Comparator**: Compare multiple units side-by-side to determine the best for your team.
- **Stats Visuals**: Beautiful visualizations of unit stats to make analysis easier.
- **Team Rater**: Rate and optimize your team configurations for maximum effectiveness.
